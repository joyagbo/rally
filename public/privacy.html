Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Rally helps you promote an event. You enter the event once, Rally writes a set of posts, and it schedules them to your social channels through Buffer. This page explains, in plain words, what Rally keeps and why.

What Rally stores

  • Your Buffer login. When you connect, Buffer gives Rally a pass that lets it create posts for you. Rally needs it to schedule your posts.
  • Your Buffer account email, timezone and channel names, so Rally can show which account and channels it will post to.

All of this is encrypted before it is saved. Your password for Buffer or for any social network is never seen by Rally.

What Rally does not store

  • Your event details and the text of your posts. They are used to build the preview in your browser and to send the posts to Buffer. Rally does not keep a copy. Buffer keeps the posts you schedule, under your Buffer account.
  • Analytics or advertising trackers. Rally has none.

Cookies

  • A short-lived cookie (10 minutes) that protects the moment you connect to Buffer.
  • A cookie that keeps you connected for up to 30 days, or until you disconnect.

Both are needed for Rally to work, so there is no cookie banner.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

  • Click Disconnect in Rally and your saved login is deleted straight away.
  • Otherwise it is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • You can also remove Rally's access at any time from your Buffer account's connected apps settings.

Who else is involved

Rally runs on Vercel (hosting) and keeps sessions in MongoDB Atlas (database), and it sends your posts to Buffer. Each of them handles data under its own terms. Rally does not sell or share your information with anyone else.
